# Music provenance

The walkthrough soundtrack is an original deterministic ambient bed generated
for Auric Artisan Terminal from mathematical sine oscillators at 110 Hz,
164.81 Hz, 220 Hz, and 329.63 Hz. It is rendered locally by FFmpeg, filtered,
and faded in and out.

It contains no downloaded recording, sample, loop, stock music, copyrighted
song, voice clone, or AI-generated audio. The exact reproducible expression is
stored in `test/marketplace/render-terminal-walkthrough.ps1`.
